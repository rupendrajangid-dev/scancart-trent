The pitch

A new checkout. One small change to make it happen

ScanCart is a new capability across your brands — the cart, the live stock lookup, the recommendations, the pricing and the checkout are all new, built and deployed by Tag ID. The one thing that isn't new is the tag: it already carries a QR code, printed on every garment. Today that code does exactly one thing — open a homepage.

  • The physical tag doesn't change — no new design, no reprint of stock already on the floor. Only the QR's destination URL changes.
  • Everything a shopper touches — cart, stock, recommendations, pricing, checkout — is a new system we build and run.
  • That new system integrates with the RFID and EPC stock reads your stores and DC already run, and with your exit hardware.

"Today, the QR does one thing: open a homepage. That's it."

The tag, close up

Everything on it stays. One field behind the QR changes.

These are two of your actual tags, reproduced field for field — desc, style, barcode, address block, MRP. Different formats, same principle: nothing here changes except one line — where the QR points.
